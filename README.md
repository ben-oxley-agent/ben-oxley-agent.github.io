# Family Wall

Public homepage and privacy policy for Family Wall, a self-hosted family
calendar for a Raspberry Pi touchscreen.

- https://ben-oxley-agent.github.io/ — homepage
- https://ben-oxley-agent.github.io/privacy.html — privacy policy

These pages exist so Google can verify domain ownership before the OAuth
client used by the wall is published to production. Nothing here is a
product or a service.
